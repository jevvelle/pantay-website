About PANTAY

Who we are

Established in 2016 and SEC-registered in 2022, PANTAY is a duly recognized, multi-awarded organization of young LGBTIQ+ individuals and allies advocating for gender equality, human rights, and inclusive democracy in the Philippines.

Organization

Our story

Established in 2016 and legally registered in 2022, PANTAY is a youth-led lobbying community advocating for gender-transformative and youth-responsive legislation in the Philippines. As a youth-led nonprofit organization, PANTAY is composed of Filipino youth of diverse SOGIESC, and student-led -based organizations, community-based groups, and other sectoral formations promoting equality, respect for human rights, nondiscrimination, and nonviolence.

Vision, Mission, Values

What we stand for

Vision

PANTAY envisions a Philippine society:

  • where every queer Filipino is free from discrimination and violence
  • where Filipinos have equal access to opportunities and services regardless of a person’s sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, and sex characteristics (SOGIESC)
  • where the Filipino LGBTIQ+ community reclaims political, economic and cultural spaces
  • where local LGBTIQ+ movements will thrive and flourish

Mission

PANTAY’s mission is to organize and mobilize communities through agenda setting, community education, and provision of direct services; educate and develop leaders as a means to create and strengthen alliances and networks; and localize and institutionalize policies and reforms.
