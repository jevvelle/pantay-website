Youth-Led · SOGIESC Equality · Philippines

10 years of Empowering Young People and LGBTIQ+ Filipinos to Advance Safer, More Inclusive Communities.

Established in 2016 and SEC-registered in 2022, PANTAY is a duly recognized, multi-awarded organization of young LGBTIQ+ individuals and allies advocating for gender equality, human rights, and inclusive democracy in the Philippines.

Our Programs

01GEIS

Gender Equality Index in Schools

An initiative by PANTAY, Pride PH, and CHR that developed the Rainbow Report Card and Rainbow School Kit to assess and strengthen SOGIESC diversity and inclusion in Philippine academic institutions.

Learn about GEIS 02POWER!

Progressive Officials Working for Equality and Rights

PINOY LGBTIQ+ POWER! is PANTAY’s flagship leadership fellowship and the Philippines’ first—and Asia’s only—national network of local LGBTIQ+ elected officials.

Learn about POWER! 03Impact

PANTAY 1 Decade Impact

For 10 years, PANTAY has built the people, organizations, evidence, and political leadership needed to turn equality from an advocacy demand into action in schools, communities, local governments, and national policy spaces.
