PANTAY 1 Decade Impact

Ten years of building people, organizations, evidence, and political leadership.

[PHOTO] Article photo

For 10 years, PANTAY has built the people, organizations, evidence, and political leadership needed to turn equality from an advocacy demand into action in schools, communities, local governments, and national policy spaces.
