What We Do

Programmatic Pillars

Three pillars guide PANTAY’s work, from national policy to community organizing to political participation.

Pillar 1

SOGIESC National Policy Advocacy and Local Campaigns

[PHOTO] SOGIESC National Policy Advocacy and Local Campaigns

As a dynamic lobbying network, PANTAY continues to advocate for the passage of gender-transformative and youth-responsive laws—advocating for anti-discrimination protections, marriage equality, anti-hate crime legislation, and legal gender recognition. With unwavering dedication, PANTAY also engages national government agencies to push for inclusive executive equality and non-discrimination policies, programs, projects, and activities (PPAs), particularly in Pride organizing, gender and development (GAD) initiatives, economic empowerment (EE), health and other social services. Central to its mission is building bridges of solidarity and intersectional allyship across diverse sectors, while empowering local advocates to lead meaningful policy change and advance gender-responsive reforms in their communities. With hope as its compass, PANTAY envisions a future where equality, dignity, and justice are realities for all.

Programs and projects

  • Equality4All: Equality Efforts for Alliance-Building and Local Lobbying
  • EquAllies (Equality Allies): Developing Intersectional Equality Ally-ship
  • Understanding Young Filipinos’ Perception on the LGBTQIA+ Community and the SOGIE Equality Bill, a Media Scan and Focus Group Study
  • ENGAGE: Engaging Non-supporters and Generating Allyship for Gender Equality through Social Media Visual Identifiers
  • LGUs For Equality: Developing Local Anti-Discrimination Ordinance Monitoring Toolkits in 6 Local Government Units and Contributing to the SOGIE Equality Bill Campaign in the Philippines
  • Project CLASH: Capacitating Youth Leaders for the Advancement of Sexual Health through Digital Interventions in selected areas in the Philippines (October 2023 to August 2024)
  • iCARE: Integration of Comprehensive Approaches to Rights-based Education and Services on Sexual, Reproductive, and Mental Health and Rights in Selected Schools in Metro Manila, Philippines
  • Project ACCESS: Active Student-Champions of a Campaign for Empowerment on SRHR in Schools
  • PROJECT GEIS (Gender Equality Index for Schools): Developing a Monitoring and Evaluation Index for SOGIESC Diversity and Inclusion for Educational Institutions in the Philippines
  • PROJECT GEIS Year 2: Expansion of Implementation of the Rainbow Report Card and Development of the Rainbow Handbook

Pillar 2

Community Organizing on Education, Economic, and Health

[PHOTO] Community Organizing on Education, Economic, and Health

Rooted in its beginnings as a network of diverse formations, PANTAY remains committed to community organizing and organizational development, believing in the transformative power of grassroots movements to drive gender equality and justice. Through capacity-building and empowering workshops, PANTAY equips advocates with the knowledge, skills, and tools to lead with confidence and compassion. With a strong focus on intersectional feminism, the organization envisions thriving, inclusive communities of care—where homophobia, transphobia, misogyny, ageism, and ableism are dismantled, not only in society at large but within movements themselves. Guided by hope and solidarity, PANTAY continues to nurture spaces where everyone can belong, grow, and lead. In sustaining this effort, PANTAY engages in building strong partnerships with national government agencies and private institutions, paving the way for greater economic inclusion and the promotion of SOGIESC-sensitive policies that uplift and empower every LGBTQIA+ persons.

Programs and projects

  • Beks to Basics
  • Students for Equality
  • Rise for Equality
  • Student and Community-based Organizational Development
  • Forging Intersectional Feminist Futures (FIFF) Community of Care
  • WERQ: Workers’ Empowerment for Responsive Queer Inclusion
  • Free to Be Me Philippines

Pillar 3

Inclusive Democracy, and Queer Political Participation and Empowerment

[PHOTO] Inclusive Democracy, and Queer Political Participation and Empowerment

PANTAY is dedicated to advancing an inclusive Philippine democracy by enhancing the political participation and empowerment of LGBTIQ+ individuals, especially the youth. The organization provides individuals and groups with the resources, knowledge, and skills necessary for feminist, inclusive and SOGIESC leadership, as well as local transformative governance. This initiative is rooted in the belief that active engagement of queer constituents in community life is essential for fostering consensus-building and driving positive change towards equality. PANTAY seeks to also formalize an organized network of advocates and elected officials who can influence local and national legislation for gender-responsive reforms, and who are empowered to take on local political roles, contributing to the organization and movement-building of local LGBTIQ+ groups.

By facilitating the institutionalization of local policies that protect LGBTQ+ rights, PANTAY aims to create a more equitable and just society where all individuals, regardless of their gender identity or sexual orientation, can actively participate and thrive in public life.

Programs and projects

  • Pinoy LGBTIQ+ POWER (Progressive Officials Working for Equality and Rights)! (June 2024 to February 2025)
  • The Rainbow AGENDA (Advancing Gender Equality Narratives to Drive LGBTIQ+ Awareness for the 2025 Philippine Midterm Elections)
